You are an expert interpreter of aerial photographs of forests.

You are given {n_images} aerial images (USDA NAIP) of the same {edge_m:.0f} m by {edge_m:.0f} m area in {location}. North is up. {layout_note} The acquisition dates, in time order, are:
{date_list}
{band_note}

A thin yellow outline marks the same candidate area in every image. An automatic method flagged it as a possible forest disturbance. Many candidates are false alarms.

Task: describe the tree canopy inside the outline on every date, so that each loss of canopy between two consecutive dates is recorded. Canopy may be lost in one interval, in several intervals (for example a harvest in stages, or salvage after a storm), or never. Loss counts when it covers about 0.1 ha or more, even if that is only part of the outline.

Differences that are not canopy loss: colour, brightness or haze differences between flights, different shadow directions or lengths, seasonal colour, small shifts in image registration, crowns leaning in different directions, regrowth, and land that was already non-forest (fields, water, existing roads and buildings) or already open on the first date.

Give:
- "states": a list with one entry per date, in date order. Each entry is one of
  "forest" (tree canopy inside the outline is intact, with no loss since the previous date),
  "partial_loss" (canopy was lost since the previous date, and substantial tree cover remains inside the outline),
  "full_loss" (since the previous date nearly all trees inside the outline were removed or killed),
  "open" (no new loss since the previous date, but the area is still open or regrowing after an earlier loss, or it was not forest to begin with).
  The first date can only be "forest" or "open".
- "affected_share": share of the outlined area that lost canopy over all dates: "none", "under_25", "25_to_75" or "over_75".
- "change_type": the type of the first loss, one of
  "clearcut" (harvest that removed nearly all trees in a unit),
  "partial_harvest" (harvest that left substantial tree cover: selection, shelterwood, patch or group cuts, visible skid trails),
  "thinning" (even removal of some trees, canopy stays mostly closed),
  "wind" (blowdown: irregular patches, downed stems, no harvest roads),
  "fire", "insect_disease" (standing dead trees, grey or red crowns),
  "gap_mortality" (a small natural gap where a few trees died or fell),
  "flood", "clearing_infrastructure" (permanent clearing for a road, building, pipeline, powerline, farm field or development),
  "mining", "other", or null when there is no loss.
- "outline_fit": how the outline fits the disturbed area: "about_right", "too_large", "too_small", "wrong_shape", or null.
- "confidence": 3 (certain), 2 (probable) or 1 (possible).
- "reason": at most 25 words on what you see.

Return only JSON in this form:
{{"states": ["forest", "forest", "full_loss", "open"], "affected_share": "over_75", "change_type": "clearcut", "outline_fit": "about_right", "confidence": 2, "reason": "..."}}
