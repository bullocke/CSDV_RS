Labelled events of each type, picked across places. Left is the image before, right the image after with the label's outline. Click one to open it on the map.

These are training labels for a model that maps forest canopy loss between two NAIP aerial images. Each place holds five to fifteen years of imagery at 0.6 m, shown here at a coarser resolution.

Candidate areas of loss came from four sources: the change in canopy height between years, two weaker height signals, and an earlier version of the model. Two vision models then looked at each candidate on the images, one candidate per request, and said whether canopy was lost, in which interval, and by what cause.

Loss is a candidate that both models accepted. Its colour is the cause when both agree, and grey when they do not. A dashed coloured line means the models disagreed on the interval.

Background is a candidate that both models rejected with confidence. The training treats it as no loss in that interval. Unlabelled candidates got a weak rejection or split the models, and the training ignores them.

The outline of a label can be the candidate's own envelope and is then wider than the loss itself. Click a label to see how it was read.

An event that lies in several sampling windows is counted and drawn once.
